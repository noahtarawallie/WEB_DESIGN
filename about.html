ABOUT THE AGENCY

Learn more about our mission, history, and achievements in developing football talent in Sierra Leone

GENERAL INFORMATION

The Football Academy of Sierra Leone is the premier institution for developing young football talent nationwide. We provide comprehensive training programs for aspiring athletes.

Our programs are designed to nurture both athletic skills and personal development. Learn more about our training methodology and how we're shaping the future of Sierra Leonean football.

We maintain state-of-the-art facilities and work with experienced coaches to ensure our players reach their full potential.

❶

AGENCY PARTNERS

We collaborate with various national and international organizations to provide the best opportunities for our players. These partnerships help us access advanced training techniques and competitive platforms.

Our network includes sports federations, educational institutions, and corporate sponsors. View our complete partner directory to see all organizations supporting our mission.

Through these collaborations, we've established exchange programs and international training camps for our most promising athletes.

❷

AGENCY PERSONNEL

Our team consists of experienced coaches, sports scientists, and administrative professionals dedicated to football development. Each staff member brings unique expertise to our academy.

We prioritize continuous professional development for our personnel. Meet our coaching staff and learn about their qualifications and experience in player development.

Our personnel maintain certifications from international football associations and regularly participate in training workshops worldwide.

❸

AGENCY HISTORY

Founded in 2010, the Football Academy of Sierra Leone has grown from a small training center to a nationally recognized institution. Our journey reflects the growing passion for football in Sierra Leone.

Over the years, we've expanded our facilities and programs significantly. Explore our historical timeline to see key milestones in our development and growth.

From our humble beginnings with just 20 trainees, we now work with over 500 young athletes annually across multiple age categories.

❹

AGENCY ACHIEVEMENTS

Our academy has produced numerous players who have gone on to successful professional careers both domestically and internationally. We take pride in their accomplishments.

Our teams have won multiple national youth championships and international tournaments. See our trophy cabinet and read about our most memorable victories.

Beyond competitive success, we've been recognized for our contribution to community development and youth empowerment through sports.
